Paris, France

Paris is my favorite city because of its beautiful architecture, rich history, and romantic atmosphere. It is the capital of France and is famous for its art, fashion, culture, and delicious food.

One of the most famous landmarks in Paris is the Eiffel Tower. Other popular attractions include the Louvre Museum and Notre-Dame Cathedral. I would love to visit Paris someday and experience its beautiful streets, historic buildings, and unique culture.
